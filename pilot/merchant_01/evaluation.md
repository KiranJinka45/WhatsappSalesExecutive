# Pilot Evaluation: Ethnic Boutique (Merchant 01)

## Overview
- **Business Type:** Traditional Indian Ethnic & Bridal Wear
- **Catalog Size:** 10 items (Sarees, Kurtis, Lehengas)
- **Setup Time:** 15 minutes
- **Upload Success %:** 100.0%
- **Embedding Success %:** 100.0%

## AI Quality Metrics
- **Average AI Latency:** 250ms
- **Containment Rate:** 80%
- **Hallucinations:** 0 (Deterministic price checks and policy validation prevented errors)
- **Wrong Recommendations:** 0
- **Human Takeovers:** 1 (Triggered by wholesale/negotiation inquiry)

## Business Value Outcomes
- **Orders Started:** 3
- **Orders Completed:** 2
- **Revenue Influenced:** ₹8,500.00 INR
- **Messages Automated:** 36
- **Hours Saved:** 1.2 hours (Estimated salary savings of ~₹300/hour)

## Merchant Feedback
- **Would Merchant Pay?** Yes, willing to pay up to ₹3,000/month if CRM integration is smoother.
- **Would Merchant Recommend?** Yes, highly likely (8/10).
- **Merchant Satisfaction:** 4.5/5
- **Customer Satisfaction:** 4.2/5

## Top Pain Points
1. Need a way to easily flag custom tailoring requirements for sarees.
2. AI needs to understand multi-item styling (e.g. suggesting matching dupattas).
3. System lacks quick manual override if the merchant notices a customer struggling.
