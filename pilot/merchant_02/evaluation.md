## Top Pain Points
1. Customer size profiles differ drastically between oversized and regular fit shirts. AI must clarify fits explicitly.
2. Exchange flows are currently escalated manually. Needs an automated webhook action.
3. Limited payment method support (prefers instant UPI confirmation via Razorpay).
